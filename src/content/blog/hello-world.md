## A new room of my own

I've spent years writing things into other people's boxes — Slack channels,
Notion docs, Google Docs that get archived two quarters later. This is an
experiment in writing in a place that's *mine*. Slower, longer, less
algorithmic.

The aesthetic here is borrowed from a study card I made for myself while
learning algorithms — warm cream paper, a fixed dot grid, *Cormorant
Garamond italic* for emphasis, DM Mono for the small mechanical labels.
It feels like the inside of a notebook. That's on purpose.

## What lives here

- **Notes** on things I'm building or breaking
- **Reading** I want to come back to
- The occasional **Daydragons** post — a running diary of one ranked
  League of Legends climb, mostly as Janna

There's no schedule. There's no newsletter. There's no analytics. If you
end up here through a link, hello. If you stay, even better.

## A small print

Posts are markdown files in [`src/content/blog`](https://github.com/kassarl/daydragons-personal-website/tree/main/src/content/blog).
The schema lives in `src/content/config.ts`. To draft a new post run
`/new-post` inside Claude Code, or copy this file's frontmatter.
